# TOLETA — Refund Policy

**EASY SHOPPING · EVERYWHERE · TRUSTED & SECURE**

_Last updated: January 2026_

---

We want you to shop with confidence on Toleta. If something isn't right with your order, this policy explains when and how you can request a refund.

---

## 1. Eligibility for Refunds

You may request a refund if:

- The item received is significantly different from the description.
- The item arrived damaged or defective.
- You received the wrong item.
- The item was never delivered.
- The vendor cancelled your order after payment.

---

## 2. Timeframe for Requests

Refund requests must be submitted within **48 hours** of delivery or within **7 days** if the order was never delivered. Requests submitted after this window may not be accepted.

---

## 3. Non-Refundable Items

The following are typically **NOT** refundable:

- Perishable goods (fresh food, produce) once accepted in good condition.
- Personal care items that have been opened or used.
- Digital downloads and gift cards.
- Custom-made or personalized items.
- Items clearly marked "final sale" or "non-refundable" at checkout.

---

## 4. How to Request a Refund

### Step 1 — Open the Toleta app
Go to your Orders section and locate the order you want refunded.

### Step 2 — Tap "Request Refund"
Select the reason and add a short description. Attach photos if the item is damaged or incorrect.

### Step 3 — Wait for review
The vendor and/or Toleta support will review your request within **2 business days**.

### Step 4 — Outcome
If approved, the refund will be processed to your original payment method or Toleta wallet within **3–7 business days**.

---

## 5. Vendor Response

Vendors are expected to respond to refund requests within 2 business days. If the vendor does not respond or you disagree with their decision, you may escalate to Toleta Support, and we will make the final decision based on evidence.

---

## 6. Refund Method

Approved refunds will be issued to:

- The original payment method (mobile money, bank card, etc.), **OR**
- Your Toleta Wallet balance, if you prefer faster processing.

Refund processing time may vary by payment provider.

---

## 7. Delivery Fees

- If the refund is due to vendor error (wrong/damaged item), the full amount including delivery fee is refunded.
- If the refund is due to buyer's change of mind and the vendor accepts, the delivery fee may be non-refundable.

---

## 8. Chargebacks

Please contact Toleta Support before initiating a chargeback with your bank or payment provider. Unauthorized chargebacks may result in account suspension.

---

## 9. Repeat Refunds

Accounts that repeatedly request refunds may be flagged for review. Fraudulent refund requests will result in account termination.

---

## 10. Vendor Payout Adjustments

When a refund is approved, the corresponding amount is deducted from the vendor's pending balance or future payouts.

---

## 11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about refunds, please contact:

- **Email:** [support@toleta.co.tz](mailto:support@toleta.co.tz)
- **In-app:** Profile → Help & Support
- **Website:** [toleta.co.tz](https://toleta.co.tz)

We aim to respond to all refund inquiries within 24 hours.
