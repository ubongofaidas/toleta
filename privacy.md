# TOLETA — Privacy Policy

**EASY SHOPPING · EVERYWHERE · TRUSTED & SECURE**

_Last updated: January 2026_

---

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Toleta collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Toleta, you consent to the practices described here.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

- **Account details:** name, email, phone number, password.
- **Business details (vendors):** business name, shop information, location.
- **Order information:** products purchased, delivery address, payment method.
- **Communications:** messages you send to support or to other users.

### Information We Collect Automatically

- **Device information:** device type, operating system, unique device identifiers.
- **Usage data:** pages viewed, features used, actions taken, time spent.
- **Log data:** IP address, access times, app crashes.
- **Location:** approximate location (based on IP or device permission) to show nearby shops.

### Information From Third Parties

- Payment processors (payment confirmations).
- Authentication providers (if you sign in with Google or similar).

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Create and manage your account.
- Process orders and payments.
- Connect buyers with vendors and coordinate delivery.
- Send notifications about orders, payouts, and account activity.
- Improve the Platform, fix bugs, and develop new features.
- Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity.
- Comply with legal obligations.

---

## 3. Information Sharing

We do **NOT** sell your personal information. We may share data with:

| Recipient | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| Vendors | To fulfill your order (name, delivery address, phone). |
| Payment processors | To process transactions securely. |
| Delivery partners | To deliver your order. |
| Service providers | Hosting, analytics, notifications, and customer support tools. |
| Legal authorities | When required by law or to protect rights and safety. |

## 4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including:

- Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
- Hashed passwords (we never store them in plain text).
- Access controls limiting who can view your data.
- Regular security reviews.

No system is 100% secure. Please use a strong, unique password and keep your account details private.

---

## 5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. When you delete your account, we remove or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

You have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you.
- Correct inaccurate information.
- Request deletion of your account and data.
- Opt out of marketing communications.
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise these rights, contact us at [support@toleta.app](mailto:support@toleta.app).
